# Photography of the 19th Century

In the 19th Century, rapid innovation caused a succession of
many different techniques for recording images onto a variety
of substances.  The three that were most popular are:

* [Daguerreotype photos](daguerreotypes.md), which were invented in 1839.

* [Ambrotype photos](ambrotypes.md), which became popular in the mid-1850s,
because they required less exposure time and were cheaper to produce.

* [Tintype photos](tintypes.md) required even shorter exposure times
and were cheaper to produce than ambrotypes.  They first became
available about the same time as ambrotypes, and largely replaced
them by the time the Civil War had ended.
