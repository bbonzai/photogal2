# Photography from the 21st Century

Plenty of pictures *should* be available from this period, since
everyone has a cell phone capable of taking and transmitting 
high-quality pictures.

We've selected two groups of photos:

* some Lewisboro-centric from the period on and just after [9/11](911.md).

* some more [recent photos](anythingelse.md) memorializing special events of the 21st Century.
